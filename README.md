# 🚀 Nomad's Forge — Universal Companion Suite for No Man's Sky

**A next-generation, community-driven companion toolkit for explorers of infinite galaxies.**
Built from the ground up in modern C++ with a focus on stability, extensibility, and a beautifully responsive experience — because the universe is big, and your toolkit should keep up.

---

## 📖 Overview

Welcome, traveler. You've just stumbled into **Nomad's Forge**, an open-source companion suite designed for the wandering souls of No Man's Sky. Whether you're charting hyperlanes through unexplored systems, tuning your exosuit for the harsh atmospheres of a frozen moon, or simply managing an ever-growing fleet of starships, Nomad's Forge was conceived as a quiet, reliable co-pilot that sits beside you — never intrusive, always ready.

Unlike conventional trainers that chase features at the cost of stability, Nomad's Forge is engineered like a **digital multitool**: modular, minimal on resources, and respectful of the game's underlying systems. Every module is opt-in, every adjustment is transparent, and every update is documented for the community.

This project grew out of a simple question: *"What if a companion tool felt less like a cheat panel and more like a genuine enhancement layer?"* Nomad's Forge is our answer.

## ✨ Feature Highlights

Here's a hand-picked tour of what makes Nomad's Forge stand apart from the crowded field of galaxy-side utilities.

### 🧭 Exploration & Navigation
- **Constellation Mapper** — visualizes your discovered systems in a clean, color-coded overlay.
- **Jump Range Advisor** — helps you plot hyperdrive routes without tedious manual math.
- **Warp Cell Planner** — calculates the optimal fuel mix based on your current inventory.
- **Beacon Synchronizer** — organizes custom waypoints across multiple saves.

### 🛠️ Inventory & Crafting
- **Stack Optimizer** — detects inefficient stack layouts and suggests tidier alternatives.
- **Recipe Browser** — a fully searchable database of refinable and craftable items.
- **Blueprint Tracker** — keeps a persistent log of what you've unlocked across playthroughs.
- **Refiner Loop Preview** — see multi-step refinement chains at a glance before committing.

### 🚀 Fleet & Starship Management
- **Fleet Roster Panel** — a clean overview of every frigate, with sortable stats.
- **Ship Comparator** — side-by-side evaluation of stats, slots, and class.
- **Expedition Timer** — unobtrusive countdowns for ongoing fleet missions.
- **Cargo Audit** — identifies redundant or low-value items taking up precious slots.

### 🎨 Interface & Experience
- **Responsive UI** — adapts gracefully across resolution and DPI settings, from handheld displays to ultrawide monitors.
- **Multilingual Support** — interface strings available in a growing set of languages, with community contributions welcome.
- **Theme Engine** — light, dark, and high-contrast palettes, all with adjustable accent hues.
- **24/7 Community Support** — our Discord and issue tracker are monitored around the clock by volunteers across every timezone.

### ⚙️ Under the Hood
- **Modern C++ Core** — written in C++20 with clear separation between logic and presentation.
- **Plugin Architecture** — third-party modules can be added without touching core code.
- **Low Overhead** — designed to coexist with the game process without competing for resources.
- **Deterministic Builds** — reproducible compilation scripts for consistency across contributors.

## 🖥️ Responsive UI Philosophy

A companion tool should never feel like a popup ad. Nomad's Forge uses a fluid layout engine that scales from compact widgets to full-screen dashboards, remembers your last configuration, and never steals focus from the game unless you ask it to. The interface is deliberately understated: muted tones, generous spacing, and typography that stays readable during long exploration sessions.

Responsiveness isn't just about pixels — it's about **attention**. Every panel fades, every notification waits its turn, and nothing blinks unless it truly matters.

## 🧠 Design Principles

Every decision in Nomad's Forge flows from four guiding principles:

1. **Respect the source.** We enhance the experience; we don't fight the game.
2. **Clarity over cleverness.** Readable code beats clever code, every time.
3. **Modular by default.** If a feature can't be removed cleanly, it doesn't belong in core.
4. **Community as co-author.** Contributors shape the roadmap as much as maintainers do.
